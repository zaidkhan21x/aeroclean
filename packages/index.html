Packages & pricing

Three packages. Clear starting prices.

Choose the package closest to the size of your ventilation system. Every price is a starting price in US dollars, and we confirm the exact scope and final price with you before any work begins.

Packages

Package 01

Small Vent

Starting at

$100USD

Designed for smaller ventilation systems or smaller service requirements.

Suitable for: Studios, apartments, single rooms or a handful of vents that need attention — for example, a few dusty registers or one problem area.

Typical scope

  • Pre-service walk-through of the vents in scope
  • Supply registers and return grilles removed, cleaned and refitted
  • Dust and debris vacuumed from each vent opening and the accessible section behind it
  • Floor and furniture protection at each work area
  • Before-and-after photos of cleaned vents on request
  • Brief summary of what was cleaned and anything we noticed

May need assessment or a quote

  • Vents beyond the number agreed for your booking
  • Main duct trunk lines
  • Vents in hard-to-reach locations such as high ceilings, attics or crawl spaces
Request Small Vent

Package 02

Medium Vent

Starting at

$240USD

Designed for medium-sized ventilation systems or larger cleaning requirements.

Suitable for: Typical homes or units where most supply and return vents need cleaning, or a smaller system with heavier dust buildup.

Typical scope

  • Everything in Small Vent, across a larger number of vents
  • Agitation cleaning of accessible branch duct runs with brushes or air tools
  • Vacuum collection of loosened dust and debris
  • Return-air grille and filter-slot area cleaned where accessible
  • Clean-up of every work area before we leave

May need assessment or a quote

  • Main trunk lines with limited access
  • Heavy renovation or construction debris
  • Properties with more than one HVAC system
Request Medium Vent

Package 03

Large Vent

Starting at

$340USD

Designed for larger ventilation systems or more extensive cleaning requirements.

Suitable for: Larger homes, multi-level properties or systems with heavier buildup across many vents.

Typical scope

  • Everything in Medium Vent, across a larger scope
  • Accessible main trunk sections cleaned where safe access already exists
  • Additional time allowed for heavier dust and debris
  • Written summary of the work, including anything that may need a specialist

May need assessment or a quote

  • Additional systems or zones beyond the agreed scope
  • Work that would require new access openings
  • Commercial or multi-unit properties

Final price

What can change the price.

Starting prices assume straightforward access and typical buildup. These are the factors we look at when we confirm your quote.
System configuration
Number of HVAC systems or zones and how the ductwork is laid out.
Number & location of vents
How many supply and return vents are in scope, and where they are.
Accessibility
Attics, crawl spaces, high ceilings or vents behind fixed furniture.
Level of buildup
Light dust versus heavy debris, pet hair or renovation dust.
Property requirements
Parking, building access rules, working-hour restrictions or travel distance.
Optional services
Dryer vent cleaning, added only if you ask for it.

Not included in any package

Unless separately agreed in writing.

  • Cleaning of HVAC equipment (coils, blower assemblies, drain pans, furnace or AC cabinets)
  • Chemical biocides, sanitizers, fogging or duct sealants
  • Mold remediation, asbestos work, pest removal or other specialist services
  • Repairs, replacement parts, filters (unless you supply them) or electrical work
