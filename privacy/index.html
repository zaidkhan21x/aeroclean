1.Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Aeroclean, trading as AERO CLEAN (“we”, “us”, “our”), handles personal information when you visit this website or contact us about our vent and air duct cleaning services.

In short: this website does not use contact forms, analytics, advertising pixels or tracking cookies. We only receive personal information that you choose to send us — for example by calling or emailing us — plus the standard technical records our website host keeps to deliver the site securely.

2.Information we collect

We collect two kinds of information:

  • Information you give us when you contact us or book a service (sections 3 and 4).
  • Limited technical information processed by our website host when your browser loads the site (section 5).

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information, and we ask that you do not send it to us (for example, Social Security numbers, health information or full payment card numbers by email).

3.Personal information you provide

When you contact us, request a quote or book a service, you may give us:

  • your name, phone number and email address;
  • the address of the Property where the service will take place;
  • details about the Property and its ventilation system, including photos you choose to send;
  • your preferred dates and times, and any access instructions;
  • billing details needed to invoice you and record payment.

We do not take payments through this website. If you pay us by card or another electronic method, the payment is handled by the payment provider you use, under its own privacy policy.

4.Contact and inquiry information

The “Call”, “Email” and similar buttons on this website open your own phone or email app. Nothing is submitted to us through the website itself. Your message is delivered by your email or phone provider and is received in our business email account or on our business phone.

We use these messages to respond to you, prepare quotes, schedule and carry out services, and keep a record of what was agreed.

5.Technical information and analytics

We do not use analytics or advertising tools on this website — no Google Analytics, no Meta Pixel, no session recording and no third-party tracking scripts.

Like almost every website, this site is delivered by a hosting provider (Hostinger). When your browser requests a page, the host’s servers automatically process technical information such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested, the referring page and the date and time. The host uses this information to deliver the website, keep it secure and prevent abuse, and may keep it in server logs for a limited period under its own policies.

If we ever add analytics or similar tools, we will update this policy first and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent before they run.

6.Cookies and similar technologies

This website does not set cookies and does not use local storage, tracking pixels or similar technologies to identify you. Our fonts are stored on our own website, so loading a page does not connect your browser to Google Fonts or other font services. Because no non-essential cookies or trackers are used, the website does not display a cookie-consent banner.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control: because we do not track visitors across websites or sell or share personal information for targeted advertising, there is nothing for these browser signals to switch off on this website. We would honor them if that ever changed.

7.How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • respond to your questions and prepare quotes;
  • schedule, carry out and follow up on services;
  • issue invoices and receipts and keep business, tax and accounting records;
  • handle concerns, complaints, damage reports or disputes;
  • keep our staff, customers and properties safe; and
  • comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use it for targeted advertising, and we do not send you marketing messages unless you ask us to.

8.How information is stored

Information you send us is kept in our business email account, on our business phone and in the business tools we use to schedule and invoice work. Access is limited to the people who need it to provide our services.

9.Information sharing

We share personal information only:

  • with service providers that help us run the business (such as email, phone and invoicing providers), who may use it only to provide their services to us;
  • with people you ask us to share it with, such as a landlord or property manager;
  • when required by law, legal process or to protect rights, property or safety; and
  • if the business is sold or transferred, with the new owner, who must continue to protect it.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not “share” it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

10.Third-party services

  • Website hosting: Hostinger — see section 5.
  • Your own email, phone or messaging apps: when you use a contact button, your provider handles the message under its own privacy policy.
  • External links: links to other websites (for example the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency) are governed by those websites’ own privacy policies.

11.Data retention

We keep personal information only for as long as we need it for the purposes in this policy, and for no longer than one year. Records are deleted automatically once that period ends, except where a law (for example, tax or accounting rules) requires a specific record to be kept for longer — in which case only that record is kept, and only for the period required.

12.Data security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect personal information, such as password-protected business accounts and limiting access to people who need it. Because this website does not collect information through forms or store it in a database, there is no visitor data held on the website itself. No method of sending or storing information is completely secure, so please avoid sending sensitive information by email.

13.Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under state privacy laws — for example, California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia and a number of other U.S. states give residents rights over their personal information when a business meets that law’s thresholds. These rights can include the right to:

  • know what personal information we hold about you and receive a copy;
  • correct inaccurate information;
  • request deletion of your information;
  • opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (we do not sell or share it); and
  • appeal our decision on your request, where state law provides that right.

Whether or not a particular law applies to us, we will consider any reasonable request to access, correct or delete your information. We will not treat you differently for making a privacy request.

14.Data access and deletion requests

To make a request, email us at contact@aeroclean.sbs with the subject “Privacy request”, and tell us what you would like us to do. You may also contact us by phone using the details in section 18.

To protect you, we will verify your identity before acting on a request — usually by confirming details that match our records. You may use an authorized agent where the law allows it; we may ask for proof of their authority. We will respond within the time required by applicable law.

15.Children's privacy

This website and our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, consistent with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

16.International data transfers

We provide services in the United States, and the information we handle is generally processed in the United States. Some of our service providers (for example, email or website hosting providers) may store or process information in other countries. Where that happens, they do so under their own safeguards and policies.

17.Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy, for example if our website or business practices change. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised. Significant changes will be highlighted on this page.
